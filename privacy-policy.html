Privacy Policy

سياسة الخصوصية

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the My Blood Is Yours humanitarian initiative (“My Blood Is Yours”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information through the website and mobile application.

توضح سياسة الخصوصية هذه كيفية تعامل مبادرة دمنا واحد الإنسانية مع المعلومات من خلال الموقع الإلكتروني وتطبيق الهاتف.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Donor name.
  • Telephone number.
  • Blood type.
  • Birth year, used to verify the donor registration age range.
  • Region and town.
  • Information submitted through contact or donor-registration forms.
  • Basic contact activity records, such as the date and time when a user confirms a call or WhatsApp contact action.

3. How we use information

We use the information only to:

  • Help users find volunteer blood donors.
  • Enable direct contact between people seeking blood and donors.
  • Process requests to add, update, or remove donor information.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the service.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.

4. Public visibility of donor information

Donor names may be displayed in search results. Telephone numbers are used to enable call or WhatsApp contact actions. Users should only use this information for genuine blood-donation needs and must not misuse, copy, market, or redistribute it.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by technical service providers used to operate the platform, including cloud database and hosting services. We may also disclose information when legally required or when necessary to protect users and the service.

6. Data storage and security

The service uses cloud infrastructure to store and process information. We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve issues, or comply with legal obligations. Donor information may remain available until it is updated, deactivated, or deleted.

8. Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request:

  • Access to information associated with you.
  • Correction of inaccurate donor information.
  • Deactivation or deletion of your donor listing.
  • Answers about how your information is handled.

9. Children

The service is intended to help communities coordinate blood donation. A parent or legal guardian should assist minors when submitting personal information or contacting donors.

10. Third-party links and communication services

The service may open telephone, WhatsApp, or external website links. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
